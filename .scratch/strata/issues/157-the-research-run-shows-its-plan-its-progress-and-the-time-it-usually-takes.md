# 157: The research run shows its plan, its progress and the time it usually takes

**What to build:** The user's design note of November 5, 2026 at 23:28 PST on the Deep research tab while a run is going ("I'm researching this project. The brief shows here when I'm done." over a growing list of past-tense lines, then "I'm writing the brief." with a spinner for a minute or more): there is no sense of how many steps there are, which one this is, or how long the whole thing takes. Patterns to take (not pixels), from Mobbin: Elicit's status rail (every step listed from the start, a check on the done ones, a spinner on the current one, a one-line result under each, and "0:27 elapsed, 5 minutes estimated"); AirOps' slim progress bar over the step list with "This may take a couple of minutes"; Rox's "step 1 of 5" count. Three changes:

1. **The plan comes first.** After the `facts` step is done, the run emits one `plan` event: `{"steps": [{"id", "kind", "label"}, ...], "usual_seconds": [low, high]}`, the ordered steps the run will take (`facts`, each source it will read or replay in the registry's order, `writing`, `checking`), with the labels the source registry gives (`config/research.toml`), so the tab can list every step at once. For a stored run the sources are the kept run's reached and not-reached sources (`Run._kept_steps`'s order); for a live run they are `kit.sources()`. The usual time is two new keys in `config/thresholds.toml` under `[research]`, `usual_seconds_low` and `usual_seconds_high`, with a comment citing the workspace runs of November 6, 2026 (a written brief took 82 to 125 seconds when the writer and the judge answered promptly, and up to 285 seconds when the judge was slow; the stored sources replay at `stored_step_seconds` apart); set them to 90 and 180. The `plan` event is the one definition of the step list; the tab never invents a step. Re-record the API fixtures (`tests/record_research_api.py`) and keep the offline tests green.

2. **A progress rail on the tab.** While a run is going, in place of the current list:
   - A title line in `text-body`: "I'm researching <project name>."; under it in `text-small text-ink-muted`: "Step <n> of <total> · <m:ss> elapsed · usually <low> to <high> minutes" (minutes from the plan's `usual_seconds`, rounded to whole minutes: 2 to 3). The elapsed clock ticks each second from the run's start on the client; it is the only time the tab ever claims as a fact. When the elapsed time passes the high end, the line says "longer than usual" after the clock and the current step shows the existing `copy.steps.stillWorking` line only when no event has come for 30 seconds, as the ask box does.
   - The design system's Progress meter (DESIGN.md, "Progress meter": 8 px pill track in `surface-2`, `primary` fill, full width of the prose column), `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuemin` 0, `aria-valuemax` the number of steps and `aria-valuenow` the number done; its fill is the share of steps done, so it moves on every step and sits still while the writer works, which the row under it explains.
   - One row per planned step, in plan order, each with a 16 px lucide icon and sr-only state words: `circle-check` in `primary` for done ("done"), `loader-circle` spinning in `primary` for the one running ("in progress"), `circle` in `ink-muted` for a step to come ("to do"), and `circle-minus` in `ink-muted` for a source not reached ("not reached"). The row's title is the step's label (the source's registry label; "Project facts", "Writing the brief", "Checking every sentence against its sources" for the fixed steps) in `text-ink` once reached and `text-ink-muted` before; under it, a result line in `text-small text-ink-muted`: for a source, "read on November 5, 2026 · kept 7 items" or "kept 14 items" or "kept no items", and for one not reached, "not reached · <the step's note>"; for writing, while running, "the longest step, usually about a minute" and, done, "<n> sentences"; for checking, while running, "checked <k> of <n> sentences" counted from the stream's `sentence` and `removed` events against the step's `sentences`, and, done, the existing kept and removed words. The running row's title carries the present-tense sentence the tab shows today (`copy.research.steps.*.running`) as its sr-only announcement in one polite live region, one sentence per step change, as `Steps` does. The rows keep their height: nothing moves when a step finishes except its icon and its result line.
   - Under the rail, one line in `text-small text-ink-muted`: "Keep this tab open. If you leave, the research stops and the last brief stays." Write this only after reading `src/strata/server/research.py` and confirming that a client that disconnects ends the run before the brief is stored (the stream is cancelled with the response; no step checks for disconnects); if that is not so, write what is.
   - Build it as a new component `web/src/components/ResearchProgress.tsx`; leave `Steps.tsx` alone (the ask box keeps its no-check-marks rule). Copy lives under `research.progress` in `web/src/copy.ts`, 10th-grade, first person where Prism speaks. The print view never shows the run. The done, failed, dropped and kept-brief states keep their current words.
