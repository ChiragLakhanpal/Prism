# 89: The score task fits the nightly window: the signals replay runs weekly and in batches, and the job's window is measured

**What to build:** On the paid workspace the nightly's `score` task ran alone from October 27, 2026, 01:47 UTC (run 1086916576670871) for the whole 150-minute window (`timeout_seconds` 9000 in `bundle/nightly.yml`), after the first full run had already been cut by the same window at the transform (ticket 85). `_score` in `src/strata/lakehouse/nightly.py` does six things in a row: the time estimate scoring of every package and what-if (`strata.estimates.scoring`), the rebidding label counts, the shadow scoring, and on a live run `_score_packages` and `_replay_signals` (ticket 37's track record: about 1,050 projects a date with two warehouse statements each, about 110 minutes on the 2X-Small serverless warehouse by the second session's measure), then the monthly watch and the retrain.
