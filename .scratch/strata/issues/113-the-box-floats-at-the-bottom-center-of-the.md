# 113: The box floats at the bottom center of the page

**What to build:** The user asked that the ask box leave the top bar and float at the bottom center of the page, with the same behavior. Move `BoxBand` out of `web/src/components/TopNav.tsx` (line 461) into a floating bar that `web/src/components/Frame.tsx` renders as the page column's last part before the footer (inside `[data-page-column]`, between `main` and `Footer`), `position: sticky` at the window's bottom. So it centers in the page column, narrows with it when the evidence drawer opens and never covers the drawer, and at the end of every page sits above the disclaimer instead of over it. The box keeps everything inside it as it is (the scope chip, the "/" hint, the placeholders, the combobox and its list) and takes a look for a light background: `surface`, a 1 px `line-strong` edge (DESIGN.md's control border), 4 px corners and the popover shadow, at most 640 px wide and the window margin at each side below that, its bottom 16 px above the window's bottom edge plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, and its focus ring in `focus` now that it no longer sits on navy. No translucency and no fade above it. The One box list opens above the box at its width, at most as tall as the window less the bar and 96 px, and scrolls inside. The bar becomes one 56 px row at every width (the Menu button below 1024 px, the logo, the wordmark, and the view switch from 768 px), so `web/index.html`'s first paint drops the box row and its 108 px height, and app.css's `bar-row`, `bar-box` and `[data-box-band]` rules shrink to what the bar still holds. The box is a part that sticks at the foot: it is marked `data-sticky="bottom"`, so `web/src/lib/sticky.ts` keeps the page's bottom scroll padding at its height plus 8 px (WCAG 2.4.11); where a page's own bar already sticks at the foot (a draft's draft bar, the judging page's grade bar), the box floats 16 px above that bar and the padding counts both. The answer panel still opens at the top of the page column; after "Ask this" the page scrolls at once so its question heading sits under the bar. "/", Escape, the scope chip, the modes "Check a plan" and "Plan a package" put the box in, and "Close the answer" returning focus to the box all work as today. The box comes after the page's content in the Tab order, is its own search landmark, and "/" still reaches it from anywhere. DESIGN.md: the Top bar row loses the box, and a new Ask bar row says where the box lives and why. Nothing animates: the box is always there, and "/" is a keyboard act.
