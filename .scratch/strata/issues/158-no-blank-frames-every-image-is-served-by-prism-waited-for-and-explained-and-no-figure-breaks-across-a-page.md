Second, P161305's sheet 5 ended in a half-empty page and its field photo landed on the next page cropped. The causes, from the code: the browser loads every Sentinel-2 tile straight from EOX (`<img src={EOX url}>` in `web/src/pages/project/ResearchPrint.tsx` and `ResearchTab.tsx`, up to 72 tiles a brief) and every photo from the volume through `/api/projects/{pid}/field-photos/{id}` (`Cache-Control: private, no-store`, one volume read a request), and "Download as PDF" calls `window.print()` at once, so the PDF is taken before the images arrive; a tile that EOX throttles or a photo still loading prints as an empty frame. In print, `.research-sheet` has only `min-height: 279.4mm`, so a site whose bullets, grids and photo add up to more than a page runs onto a second page with no header, and `.photo img { height: 62mm }` crops a portrait photo to the box. Four changes:

1. **Prism serves the tiles.** A new route `GET /api/research/tiles/{year}/{zoom}/{row}/{col}.jpg` (`src/strata/server/research_tiles.py`, registered where ticket 149's routes are). It builds the EOX address from the one endpoint in `config/research.toml` (`sources.sentinel2_mosaic.endpoint`, the registry's definition of the address), answers from a per-process memory cache first, then from `strata.ops.research_cache` (the `Cache` in `src/strata/research/fetching.py`, body kept as base64 under `source_key = "sentinel2_mosaic"`; the first miss for a project may load every cached tile of that project's grids in one query), and only then fetches from EOX with the named client (`prism-research/0.1`, as every reader does, paced by the source's `pace_seconds`), keeps the answer in the cache and serves it with `Content-Type: image/jpeg` and `Cache-Control: public, max-age=86400` (the tiles are public; EOX's CC BY-NC-SA credit stays in the caption). A tile EOX refuses or that is not an image answers 502 with `{"code": "tile_server"}`. The web maps an item's EOX address to the route (`tileRoute(url)` in `web/src/lib/research.ts`, reading year, zoom, row and column out of the address) in both the tab's grids and the print's. A sibling of the seed, `ops/research/seed_tiles.py` (`--all`, `--project`, `--env-file`, `--state-file`, the same shape as `seed_photos.py`), reads each stored run's `sentinel2_mosaic` items and fetches every tile of their grids into the cache once, paced, printing how many it kept and how many EOX refused, so the demo's five projects never depend on EOX answering live. Offline, the fixture's state holds the sample's tiles for P156634 (the recorded ones under the sample's folder if they exist, or fetched once by the test recorder with the named client); the route's tests use a fake fetch.

2. **The PDF waits for its images, and says so.** "Download as PDF" no longer prints at once: the button reads "Preparing the PDF" with "<n> of <total> images loaded" beside it while every `<img>` in the print view loads (`decode()` or the load event, a failed image counting as finished), then calls `window.print()`; a cap of 25 seconds prints anyway. Copy under `research.pdf` in `web/src/copy.ts`.

3. **A frame that fails says why.** Every image in the print view and on the tab has an error handler. A tile that fails shows its cell in `surface-2` with no words (nine cells are too small), and the grid's caption gains one sentence: "<n> of the 9 tiles for <year> did not load from the tile server." (or "The tile server answered none of the 9 tiles for <year>."). A photo that fails shows its frame in `surface-2` with the words "Photo not loaded: the photo store did not answer." in `text-small text-ink-muted`, under the same caption. An image still loading when the PDF's cap expires counts as failed with "did not load in time". Nothing is ever blank without a sentence.

4. **No figure breaks across a page, and no photo is cropped.** In `web/src/styles/research.css` and `ResearchPrint.tsx`: every `img` carries its `width` and `height` attributes so the page breaks are computed before the pixels arrive; a satellite pair (`.figure`) and a photo figure are `break-inside: avoid`; a photo sits in a fixed 4:3 frame with `object-fit: contain` on `surface-2` (never `cover`); and each "By site" sheet is budgeted so it fits a Letter page: a site's sheet holds its title, its bullets and at most one satellite pair, and the site's photos go on their own sheet after it ("Field photos near <site>", up to four to a sheet, two to a row) whenever the site has a satellite pair or more than one photo; a site with no pair and one photo keeps it on its sheet.
