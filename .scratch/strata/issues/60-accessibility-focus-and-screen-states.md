# 60: Accessibility, focus, pointer behaviour and the states a screen shows, with their shots and tests

**What to build:** Beyond the WCAG 2.2 AA checks axe runs, the design reviews and verifiers of the done screen tickets found things a screen reader hears wrong or late, focus that lands on the page for a frame or moves without being asked, pointer and touch behaviour that shows the wrong tooltip or keeps one open, and states a page can reach that say something untrue for a moment, lose a paragraph, or were never recorded, captured or put in the gallery. None of it failed a criterion, so none blocked. This ticket takes them all in one worktree, each as its note gives it, with a screen test for each. It changes no number, claim or tool contract; where an item adds a recorded stream or a capture, the recording comes from the scripted cases in `tests/fixtures/agent/cases.toml` and the recording test, never by hand.

Read first: DESIGN.md section 10 (accessibility) and the Announcer, Check line, Draft bar and Segmented control rows of section 5; `web/src/components/AnswerPanel.tsx`, `Announcer`, `web/src/App.tsx` (the alerts and `VIEW_PAGES`), `web/src/components/Button.tsx`, `Table.tsx`, `TextLink` in `web/src/styles/app.css`; `web/capture/shots.ts` and `web/src/gallery/`; `tests/fixtures/agent/cases.toml` and the recording test; the notes named in the Items.
