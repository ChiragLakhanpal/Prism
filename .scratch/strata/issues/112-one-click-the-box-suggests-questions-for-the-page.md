# 112: One click: the box suggests questions for the page, and copy keys

**What to build:** (1) **Suggestions when the box is active.** A new table in the examples registry, `config/examples.toml`, `[[ask]]`, one row per suggestion: `key` (its words in `copy.box.suggest`), `page` (where it shows: `package`, `project`, `plan_check`, or `home`, which also serves every page with no scope), `benchmark` (the id of the `evals/benchmark.yaml` question it shortens, so each suggestion is a question Strata has been measured on), and for `home` rows `within` (`{demo_package}` or `{showcase}`, filled from `config/slice.toml` as Start here's links are). Tonight's rows, each the benchmark question without its as-of date, with `{id}` the page's scope: package "How long did packages like {id} take from notice to signed contract?" (find-comparables-ke-nwwda-541055), "How many packages like {id} were advertised again, and why?" (rebidding-risk-ke-nwwda-541055) and "Were packages like {id} signed above or below their cost estimates?" (compare-cost-estimates-ke-nwwda-541055); project "Which contracts were signed on {id} since its last ISR?" (project-records-p156634) and "Does {id} need a look, and which signals put it there?" In the box (`web/src/components/IntentBox.tsx`, `web/src/lib/intents.ts`, drawn by `BoxBand.tsx`): focused and empty, the list keeps its hint, then a group named by the page ("Ask about this package", "Ask about this project", "Ask about this plan", or "Ask, for example" on Home and on pages with no scope; keys `box.suggest.group.*`) with at most three options in the One box list's own option rows (lucide `message-square-text`, the question with its ID in Roboto Mono, no second line). Typing replaces them with today's options. Down, Up and Enter, or a click, choose one: its words go into the box and it is asked within the page's scope, exactly as "Ask this" asks. Nothing moves, since "/" opens the list from the keyboard. (2) **Copy keys.** A new component, `CopyKey` (`web/src/components/CopyKey.tsx`): a 24 by 24 px ghost icon button with 4 px corners and lucide `copy` at 16 px in `ink-muted`, hovering to `tint-hover`, named "Copy {what}" (`copyKey.name`), with the shared Tooltip (`web/src/components/Popover.tsx`) saying "Copy" on hover and focus. A press, Enter or Space writes the text with `navigator.clipboard.writeText`, turns the icon into lucide `check` and the tooltip into "Copied" for 2 seconds, and says "Copied {what}." once in a polite status; if the write fails, the tooltip says "I couldn't copy it. Select the text instead." Focus stays on the key, and nothing on its line moves. Three places: after the STEP reference in the package head (`web/src/components/PackageFacts.tsx`, `PackageHeader`), copying the reference; after the P-code in a project's header line (`ProjectHeader.tsx`, `ProjectHeaderLine`), copying the P-code; and in the evidence drawer's head, before the close button, copying the source as one line: its heading and its meta line's parts as shown, joined with commas instead of middle dots. The key and its ID never part at a line end (a word joiner, as `CitationMarker.tsx` uses). Not in table rows tonight: a key on every row adds a Tab stop per row. Add DESIGN.md's Copy key row and the suggestions to its One box list row.
