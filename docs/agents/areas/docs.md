# Area brief: docs

Tickets with `Area: docs` write what the panel reads: the proposal, the deck's words and speaker notes, the README, and the "designed, not built" list.

Read: `docs/assessment.md` (the brief and its rubric), `docs/PLAN.md` section 17 (the rubric map), `DESIGN.md` sections 7 and 8 (writing, attribution), and `GLOSSARY.md`.

- The proposal follows the assessment's four modules and maps each section to the rubric.
- Every number in the proposal or the deck was measured by the build; cite where it came from (a test, a benchmark run, a query).
- Plain sentences, glossary words and World Bank usage, with none of the words DESIGN.md section 7 bans. Call the Skill tool with `anthropic-skills:unslop` on every draft.
- What isn't built is labeled "designed, not built".
- The deck lives at `/present` and nothing links to it; its figures come from `design-lead`.
