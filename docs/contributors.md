# Who builds Prism

- **Chirag Lakhanpal** built Strata, the app Prism grew from: the lakehouse, the tools and the verifier, the screens, the deck and the proposal, October 22 to November 5, 2026.
- **Surya Yadav** built ProcIntel, a second app on the same brief (October 23 to November 3, 2026), and brings four of its designs to Prism as proposals: the MCP server, the timeline across a project's packages, goal seeking, and the update pipeline an admin can edit (`docs/proposals/`).

Prism is an independent demo built on World Bank open data. It is not a World Bank product, and the World Bank has not reviewed or endorsed it.
