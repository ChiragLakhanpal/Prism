            Water and Sanitation Development Project (P156634)



  Overall project progress has remained steady despite challenges experienced with disbursement of funds to counties. Total commitments (signed
  contracts and liquidity support grant) now stand at US$261 million (99%) out of the available IDA-SUF Credit of US$263 million and disbursement
  stands at US$119 million (44.8%).
  The Project remains relevant for GoK, as it contributes to the country’s goal of achieving universal water and sanitation coverage. Importantly, the
  Project provided a great opportunity for GoK’s response to the covid-19 pandemic by availing a US$50 million Conditional Liquidity Support Grant
  (CLSG) facility to 77 water services providers (WSPs) affected by the pandemic. Available data indicates that the grant facility was successful in
  cushioning WSPs’ cashflow shortages to help maintain water services during the most critical phase of the pandemic. In addition, through the
  purchase of critical operational goods and equipment, the project has supported WSPs in improving their operational performance, further
  enhancing their capacity to provide better services.
