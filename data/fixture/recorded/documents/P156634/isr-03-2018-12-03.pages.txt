Implementation Status and Key Decisions 
The Project became effective on March 9, 2018. Overall the Project is progressing in a moderately satisfactory manner.  Most of the preparatory 
activities required to be implemented before actual project implementation and disbursement, including the opening of designated/project accounts 
and establishment of Project Coordination Units (PCUs) by the different Implementing Agencies (IAs) have been completed. However, signing of

the County Participation Agreements (CPAs) between the National Treasury and Counties is still pending, and this is causing significant delays in 
project implementation because disbursement of funds to the counties can only happen after the CPAs are signed.
Despite the delays in finalizing the CPAs, most of the IAs have made good progress towards building a solid pipeline of implementable activities. 
The IAs have ready detailed designs and bidding documents for immediate interventions, for which procurement has started and in some cases is 
advanced. Some of the IAs have limited capacity and experience, therefore regular oversight and technical assistance is needed to ensure that 
these activities are procured in a timely manner and following applicable Bank procurement guidelines. The MoWS is in the process of procuring 
a framework technical assistance consultancy which is expected to provide practical hands-on procurement, FM, technical and safeguards 
management support to the IAs, and progressively build their capacity. The Bank will continue to provide support to the IAs through training on 
fiduciary and safeguards aspects as well as guidance on technical and utility reform activities to ensure the Project meets its PDO.
