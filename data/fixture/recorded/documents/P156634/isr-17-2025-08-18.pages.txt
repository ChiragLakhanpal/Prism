Original Development Objective (Approved as part of Approval package on 26-Apr-2017)
The Project Development Objective (PDO) is to improve water supply and sanitation services in select coastal and 
northeastern regions in Kenya and improve the financial performance of water service providers affected by the COVID-
19 pandemic.

4.  KEY ISSUES & STATUS
4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
•There was a significant delay in transfer of funds to the counties for the Financial Year 24/25. The County 
Government Additional Allocations Act (CGAAA) was assented to mid-May 2025, and transfer of funds from the 
Exchequer took place in July 2025. As of August 1, 2025, all the Implementing Agencies, except for Kwale County, 
had transferred funds from the respective County Revenue Funds (CRF) accounts to the Special Purpose Account 
(SPA)
•Nevertheless, some Implementing Agencies were able to proceed with works and bring the total number of 
completed works from 12 in January 2025 to 19 as of July 2025. The completed works contracts in Mombasa, 
Malindi, Garissa, Wajir, Kwale and the bulk water distribution works under the Coast Water Works Development 
Agency are already benefiting the intended populations.
