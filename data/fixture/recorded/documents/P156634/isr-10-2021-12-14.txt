 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  Although Implementing Agencies have now commenced with works activities, and have their supervision consultants on board, the project remains
  substantially behind schedule. Some originally planned activities, such as fecal sludge facilities for some of the IAs, will not be completed within the
  project timeframe. For these, the Project will support full preparation of detailed designs, bidding documents and safeguards documents in
  readiness for implementation in future operations, by the government, or by other partners.
