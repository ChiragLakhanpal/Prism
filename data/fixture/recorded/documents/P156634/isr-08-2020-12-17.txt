 Implementation Status and Key Decisions

  The Project remains relevant to Kenya’s water and sanitation sector development agenda: it will support GoK’s Vision 2030 and the “Big Four”
  Agenda and it is also supporting the Government to mitigate the spread of the Covid-19 pandemic and in building community health resilience.

  The project has been restructured twice, to help address implementation bottlenecks and to respond to the Covid-19 pandemic. The first
  restructuring, completed in May 2020, has helped to improve implementation progress: the main challenge of funds flow to the counties was
  resolved and both the project steering committee and the technical committee were constituted and operationalized. The second restructuring was
  completed in August 2020 and the first tranche of the conditional liquidity grant in support of water service providers affected by the Covid-19
  pandemic is expected to be disbursed in December 2020.

  Although the pace of implementation has picked up, the project remains substantially behind schedule. To date, contracts worth about US$60
  million (20 percent of the Credit) have been committed (signed/awarded). About half of these contracts are at the mobilization stage, although
  construction schedules have been affected by GoK restrictions related to the Covid-19 pandemic. In addition, most of the implementing agencies
  have encountered delays in awarding the contracts for consulting firms to undertake design review and supervision of the immediate works
  contracts, and to prepare detailed designs for any additional medium and long-term interventions. This has further slowed the commencement of
  the awarded works contracts. It is assessed that some originally planned activities cannot be completed within the project timeframe. The World
  Bank and the Government of Kenya are discussing an action plan, which may involve a further project restructuring to align the project scope and
  targets to the project timeframe.
