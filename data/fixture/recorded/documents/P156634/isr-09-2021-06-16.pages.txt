Implementation Status and Key Decisions
 
The Project remains relevant to Kenya’s water and sanitation sector development agenda: it will support GoK’s Vision 2030 and the “Big Four” 
Agenda and it is also supporting the Government to mitigate the spread of the Covid-19 pandemic.
The project has been restructured three times. The first restructuring was to help address implementation bottlenecks. The second restructuring 
reallocated funds to assist WSPs that were negatively affected by economic shock caused by Covid-19. The third restructuring was undertaken 
following a formal request from National Treasury (NT) in February 2021 to align the project scope and revise results framework to reflect what is 
achievable within the remaining project timelines.  
Although the pace of implementation has picked up with both commitments and disbursements increasing, the project remains substantially behind 
schedule. All but one of the IAs have awarded the contracts for the consulting firms to undertake design review and supervision of the immediate 
works contracts, and to prepare detailed designs for any additional medium and long-term interventions. 
Some originally planned activities, such as the fecal sludge facilities for some IAs, will not be completed within the project timeframe.  For these, the 
Project will support full preparation of detailed designs, bidding documents and safeguards documents so that they can be implemented should 
alternative financing be available.
