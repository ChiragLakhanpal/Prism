  Project implementation improved over the last six months following disbursements made to the Implementing Agencies that allowed for settling of
  pending bills and re-mobilization of contractors. The ripple effect of the delays is that whereas a good number of the 42 works contracts are
  expected to be completed within the project closing date, at least 33% are at high-risk of non-completion within the current timelines and would
  require very close monitoring at national and sub-national leadership levels.
