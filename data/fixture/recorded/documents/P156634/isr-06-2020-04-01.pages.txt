Implementation Status and Key Decisions
 
Overall project implementation progress has been slow. However, the main bottleneck of funds transfer to counties has been unlocked. All six 
counties under Component 1 and 2 received their first disbursement in their County Revenue Fund accounts at the end of December 2019. The 
counties subsequently transferred the funds to their water services providers (WSPs), which are in the process of making payments for signed

2020 and is awaiting countersignature by the National Treasury. 
The implementing agencies (IAs) have continued to advance procurement of activities in their approved procurement plans.  All the IAs have also 
made good progress in the procurement of consulting firms to undertake design review and supervision of the immediate works contracts, and to 
prepare detailed designs for any additional identified medium and long-term interventions. The IAs have received significant capacity building and 
training in financial management, procurement, and safeguards management to ensure that they can implement the project effectively.  The MoWSI 
is expected to sign the contract for the framework consulting firm that will provide technical assistance to the IAs on procurement, engineering, 
safeguards, financial management, and monitoring, evaluation and performance assessment soon. This technical support will further enhance the 
capacity of the IAs as well as help to monitor and coordinate the project.
