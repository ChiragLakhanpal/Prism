Implementation Status  and Key Decisions
Overall the Project is progressing in a moderately satisfactory manner.  The national and local implementing agencies are making steady progress 
towards building a solid pipeline of implementable activities and consolidating their project coordinating units. The Project became effective on March 9, 
2018. However, preparatory activities  (i.e. signing of the Country Participation Agreements and FY 2018 budget allocation) have not materialized, which 
has impeded flow of IDA funds to local and regional implementing agencies to carry out project activities. The Bank team expects the Borrower to 
complete key preparatory activities by July 31 and expects to have a strong FY 2019 implementation year. The Bank continues providing support to the 
IAs through training on fiduciary and safeguards aspects as well as guidance on technical and utility reform activities to ensure the Project meets its 
PDO during the project time period.
