4.  KEY ISSUES & STATUS
4.1 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
The project has made significant progress in the completion of critical contracts needed to attain the project’s 
development objective. Overall physical progress of works contracts is at 85 percent, with 46 percent of works contracts 
and all goods contracts completed. Several of these completed works are already benefiting the intended beneficiaries 
including water distributions works in all seven WSPs in the Project,  water kiosks in Garissa, ablution blocks in Ukunda, 
Kwale and Wajir towns, sewer connections in Mombasa and the Baricho bulk water supply scheme whose capacity has 
been expanded to serve Mombasa and Kilifi counties better and its resilience to flooding risks enhanced. The remaining 
works contracts are expected to be completed within the remaining project period.   
Grievances raised under the project are being closely monitored by both the Bank and the Ministry for their resolution 
ahead of the project closure.
