WATER AND SANITATION DEVELOPMENT PROJECT (WSDP)
SUMMARY PROCUREMENT PLAN (first eighteen months)
I. General
The following procurement plan has been developed for project implementation during the 
first eighteen months and provides the basis for the procurement methods and bank 
prior/post review thresholds.  It covers the three categories, namely, Works; Consultancies; 
and Goods. The plan will be updated when need arises to reflect the actual project 
implementation needs. 
1. Project information:  
Country / Borrower: Republic of Kenya 
Project: Water and Sanitation Development Project (WSDP) 
IDA Credit No.  6029 & 6030
Project Implementing Agencies: (a) Ministry of Water and Irrigation, (b) 
WaSREB, (c) Water Service Providers: Kilifi-Mariakani Water and Sewerage 
Company (Kimawasco),; Taita-Voi Water and Sewerage Company (Tavevo); 
Mombasa Water Supply and Sanitation Company (Mowassco); Kwale Water and 
Sewerage Company (Kwawasco); Garissa Water and Sewerage Company 
(Gawasco); Wajir Water and Sewerage Company (Wajwasco); and Malindi 
Water and Sewerage Company; and (d) Counties: Kilifi; Taita Taveta; 
Mombasa; Kwale; Garissa; and Wajir 
2. Bank’s approval Date of the First Eighteen Months’ Procurement Plan: 
June 15, 2017
3. Date of General Procurement Notice: June 14, 2017
4. Period covered by this procurement plan: June 2017 to December 2018
