Implementation Status and Key Decisions

 Overall the Project is progressing in a satisfactory manner. The two IDA credit agreements were signed on June 21, 2017. The national and local
 implementing agencies are making steady progress towards building a solid pipeline of implementable activities, consolidating their project coordinating
 units (PCUs), and finalizing the implementation arrangements to ensure adequate flow of funds from National Treasury to local and regional IA.
 Furthermore, the Bank team delivered a 3-day training on procurement, financial management and safeguards training in August 2017 to the PCUs
 staff, and several IAs are advancing procurement procedures of key activities included in their procurement plans. On October 18, 2017, the Bank
 granted an extension of effectiveness from October 21, 2017 to December 15, 2017.
