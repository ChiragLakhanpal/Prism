 Implementation Status and Key Decisions
  Overall the project is moving at a slower pace, with some progress noted since the last mission in February 2018. Implementation of Component
  A, Rural Connectivity Improvement progressed reasonably well, by completing about 97% of civil works under conventional design, including
  some progress in the ongoing pilot cost-effective technology works. It has been reported that 8 roads have been completed without obtaining the
  necessary forest clearances and there are also serious delays in processing forest clearances for another 7 roads, which is still pending with the
  forest department, and this is likely to impact completion of these works. The mission advised that without diligent coordination and urgent follow-up
  with forest department, these roads might not be completed by project closing. In Component B, Road Sector Modernization and Performance

  Enhancement, there has been progress in some activities while a substantial delay encountered in the most important subcomponent -
  Development of a Sustainable Road Asset Management System (RAMS). It is noted that the system providing consultant (SPC) is now appointed to
  develop RAMS. Out of the four activities under this component, two have successfully been completed namely: (i) Preparatory works to Improving
  Engineering Practices and Business Procedures, and (ii) Developing a Strategy for Human Resources Development. While these outputs
  demonstrate a good progress, the key outcomes PDO outcomes namely approval of Road Sector Policy and operationalization of RAMS are
  substantially delayed. Though the (i) study report to Improving Road Sector Policy, Strategy and Financing Framework has been finalized, is yet to
  be approved by the cabinet; (ii) SPC consultant has been mobilized, RAMS is yet to be completed and operational both activities will not be
  completed within the current credit closing period. In Component C, Road Safety Management, there has been a very little progress with regards
  to the Safe Corridor Demonstration Program (SCDP) since last mission in February 2018. Although preparatory activities are completed, there are
  significant delays in the procurement process of civil works and road safety equipment due to poor bid response and the resulting rebidding. These
  activities, especially SCDP civil works, are related to key outcome indicators attributed to the PDO, are substantially delayed and will not be
  completed within the current credit closing period. The road Safety Education and Awareness Program reached out to approximately 81% of the
  Gram Panchayats.

 Overall Comments                         PDO




  Good progress in terms of the submission of the road sector policy and financing framework which is under
  internal approval before its final approval by the State Cabinet, completion of (ii) Modernization of
  Engineering Practices and Business Procedures - which proposed a series of systems and business process
  improvements with automation and under consideration by the sector modernization task force; and (iii) Human
  Resource Management Strategy, which produces a HR Strategy with key recommendations that have been

  prioritized for implementation. These are some very strategic achievements for the Rajasthan Road Sector. On
  the other hand, the Road Asset Management System (RAMS) related activities, being the major activity of the
  component and attributed to the PDO, is substantially delayed. Having said that, we are pleased to report that
  the contract for SPC has been signed on August 2, 2018. The procurement for services for data collection is
  still to be procured. Implementation will start, but the PDO indicator not likely to be achieved during the
  project period.
