Implementation Status and Key Decisions

 Overall the project activities are moving at a better pace. Component A, related to the construction of rural roads is progressing satisfactorily by
 completing about 94% (awarded 96%) of civil works contracts whilst the ongoing contracts are expected to be completed well within the project
 completion timeline. Some contracts relating to traditional roads and cost effective roads are yet to be awarded but also planned to be completed within
 the project completion period. Component B related to the implementation of the sector Modernization activities, are making progress with most of the
 consultancies now awarded and in implementation phase. Also, Component C, related to Road Safety Management is also progressing satisfactorily
 with almost all procurement completed. Any further delay in the activities/ procurement of the Road Sector Modernization component and Road Safety
 component will risk achievement of the PDO within the project implementation period. Therefore, a strong focus on project management and
 supervision is required to complete all remaining activities in the next 24 months i.e. by credit closing date of December 31, 2018.
