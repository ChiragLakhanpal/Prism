Implementation Status  and Key Decisions
Overall the project is moving at a different pace.  Component A, related to the construction of rural roads is moving satisfactorily by completing a 
substantial number of civil works contracts whilst the ongoing contracts are expected to be completed in a reasonable time.  Also, Component C, related 
to Road Safety Management continued to show encouraging steps for the essential activities, while others are delayed. Component B related to the 
implementation of the sector Modernization activities, has moved faster than the previous periods in procurement but needs more effort to make up for 
the delays encountered in the past. To that effect, a strong focus in the project management and supervision is required.
