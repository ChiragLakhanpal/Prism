                         Implementation Status Overview
                         The credit has been made effective on March 10, 2014. Taking advantage of the retroactive financing provision of the credit agreement, the project has made good progress in civil
                         works; awarded 832 roads (1930km) out of the total 1043 roads (2521 km) - 73% achievement. 94 roads have been substantially completed thereby connecting 94 villages to the
                         network. The total value of civil works completed and certified is equivalent to $34 million (25%). Actual expenditure of the project as of May 19, 2014 stands at approximately $28

                         Key Decisions Regarding Implementation
Public Disclosure Copy




                         PWD needs to focus on implementation of the sector modernization and road safety components, strengthen the PMU through experts hired from the market.
                         PWD needs to adopt the PMGSY On-line Monitoring, Management and Accounting System (OMMAS) as a monitoring tool for the project. This will not only improve project
                         management but also enhance transparency and accountability to the public.
