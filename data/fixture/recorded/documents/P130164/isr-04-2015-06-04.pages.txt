Implementation Status  and Key Decisions
The credit has been made effective on March 10, 2014.The project has made good progress in civil works; awarded 1004 roads (2340km) out of the 
total 1043 roads (2521 km) - 96% achievement. 672 roads have been substantially completed thereby connecting 681 villages to the network. Actual 
expenditure of the project as of May 8, 2015 stands at approximately $116 million which equates to 66 %. Disbursement from the Credit 5310-IN as of 
May 8, 2015 stands at approx. US$ 64.9 million (43% of the allocated amount).
While the overall implementation pace of civil works is generally satisfactory, the quality of the construction of some contracts visited raises some 
concerns. Both component B and C, related to Road Sector Modernization  and Road Safety Management have shown encouraging steps for the 
essential activities, while others key activities are behind and yet at procurement stage. The following are immediate actions:
1. Functionality of the sector modernization task force, which are both legal covenant, yet to be complied with,
2. Operationalization and use of the on-line monitoring and management system, and
3. Full engagement and accountability of the PMU and PIU staff to ensure monitoring and quality control contract implementation.
