Implementation Status and Key Decisions

 Overall the project is moving at an improved pace with most of the roads completed and procurement of consultancies of component B and C (which
 were pending for a while) have been awarded in the last 6-9 months. The construction of rural roads has been progressing satisfactorily with about 90%
 of road works substantially completed. A few roads works under this component have been delayed due to unavailability of land and/or pending forest
 clearances, but it is expected that all road works will be completed well within the project completion date including those road works which are currently
 experiencing delays due to forest clearances and land unavailability issues. The Road Sector Modernization, has picked up in the past 6 months, after
 initial delays, with most of the consultancies now awarded and under implementation phase. These activities need to further speed up to be completed
 within the project implementation period. The Road Safety Management component is progressing satisfactorily with all procurement completed.
 Implementation of the Road Safety Demonstration Corridor requires close monitoring to be completed within the project completion period.
