Implementation Status and Key Decisions

 The credit has been made effective on March 10, 2014.The project has made good progress in civil works; awarded 974 roads (2340km) out of the total
 1043 roads (2521 km) - 93% achievement. 383 roads have been substantially completed thereby connecting 392 villages to the network. Actual
 expenditure of the project as of November 28, 2014 stands at approximately $66 million which equates to 34 %. Disbursement from the Credit 5310-IN
 as of November 28, 2014 stands at approx. US$ 23.33 million (i.e. 14.32% of the allocated amount).
 While the overall implementation pace of civil works is generally satisfactory, the quality of the construction of some contracts visited raises some
 concerns. Also, the component C, related to Road Safety Management has shown encouraging steps for the essential activities, while others activities
 are behind. On the other hands, the component B related to the implementation of the sector Modernization which is a key success for the project
 development objectives is delayed and remains at procurement stage for most of the activities. In addition, there is a need to improve the overall project
 management mainly data collection, monitoring and reporting, construction quality assurance and contract management is critical for a successful
 implementation. The following are immediate actions:
 1.       the recruitment of the Project Management Consultancy firm,
 2.       the functionality of the sector modernization task force, which are both legal covenant, yet to be complied with,
 3.       the operationalization and use of the on-line monitoring and management system, and
 4.       Appointment of dedicated and qualified PMU and PIU staff to ensure monitoring and quality control contract implementation.
