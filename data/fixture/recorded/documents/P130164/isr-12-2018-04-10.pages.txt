Implementation Status  and Key Decisions
The Component A related to civil works has been successful achieving for the main part the targets for civil works with 10 months remaining in the 
project. Achievements of about 99% of the (i) the number of rural villages connected; (ii) number of rural people with access to all weather roads, 
connecting 1008 villages representing 96%. The focus now however is on the asset management and enhancing maintenance management system. 
Although most of the activities under components B & C, have been completed, other critical activities under both components remain at early stage of 
procurement as far as RAMS and RSDC related activities.
