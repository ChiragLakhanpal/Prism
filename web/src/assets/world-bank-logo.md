- Source: https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/media_1b9d836624bdfd028dfbe9f018f3f12e52326504c.svg

The World Bank's terms say its logos need express written permission and must not imply an association. DESIGN.md section 9 records why the private demo shows it anyway, and the footer disclaimer stays on every screen. `web/tests/shell.spec.ts` checks that the file still matches the hash above.
